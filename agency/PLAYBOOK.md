# The Hublot Plan

**Goal:** earn enough to buy Dad a Hublot.

| Target | Price | What it takes at $499/mo per client |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-owned Classic Fusion | ~$5,000 | 3 clients × ~4 months |
| New Classic Fusion | ~$8,500 | 4 clients × ~5 months |
| New Big Bang | ~$15,000+ | 5 clients × ~6 months |

Your only jobs: **send messages, take short calls, press "post."** Everything else (captions, graphics ideas, reports, replies) Claude writes for you — just paste what you need into a Claude chat.

---

## Week 0 — Setup (about 1 hour total)

1. **Set your email** in `agency/index.html` (search for `YOUR_EMAIL`). Change `BUSINESS_NAME` if you want a different name.
2. **Put the site online (free):** GitHub Pages or Cloudflare Pages → point it at the `agency/` folder. Or drag the `agency` folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop — done in 30 seconds.
3. **Make a business Instagram** with the same name. Post 3 things: what you do, a before/after profile makeover, and the pricing.
4. **Payments:** create a Stripe or PayPal account for invoices. **Under 18? A parent/guardian must open it** — ask Dad's partner, a sibling, or another adult (keeps the surprise).
5. Make a simple list (Notes app or a spreadsheet): *Business · Handle · Date messaged · Replied? · Call? · Signed?*

---

## Every day — 30–45 minutes

1. Find **15 local businesses** on Instagram/Google Maps whose socials are weak (last post > 1 month ago, blurry photos, no posts at all). Best targets: restaurants, cafés, barbers, salons, gyms, boutiques, contractors, realtors.
2. Send each one the DM below (personalize the bold part).
3. Follow up once with anyone who didn't reply after 3 days.

Math: 15/day × 5 days = 75 messages/week → ~5–8 replies → ~2–3 calls → **~1 client/week.**

---

## Scripts (copy-paste)

### First DM
> Hi! I was checking out **[Business]** — **[one genuine compliment, e.g. "your brisket photos look unreal"]**. I noticed your page hasn't posted since **[month]**. I run social media for local businesses and I'd love to put together a free audit with 3 quick things that'd get you more customers from Instagram. No cost, no catch — want me to send it over?

### Follow-up (3 days later)
> Hey, just bumping this in case it got buried — happy to send the free audit whenever. Takes me 10 minutes and it's yours to keep either way 🙂

### Walk-in (even better — owners say yes more in person)
> Hi, is the owner around? I do social media for businesses around here. I noticed your Instagram hasn't been updated in a while — I'd love to make you a free one-page plan to get more customers from it. Can I grab your email or number to send it?

### The free audit (send when they say yes)
Paste into Claude: *"Write a friendly 1-page Instagram audit for [business], a [type] in [city]. Their profile: [paste bio, # of posts, last post date, what their photos look like]. Give 3 specific quick wins and end by offering the Growth plan at $499/mo with the first week free."*

### Call script (15 minutes)
1. "Tell me about your business — who's your ideal customer?"
2. "What's been hard about keeping up with social media?"
3. "Here's what I'd do for you the first month: [3 things from the audit]."
4. "Most businesses like yours go with Growth at $499/month. First week is free so you can see the content before paying anything. Want to start Monday?"
5. If price is an issue → offer Starter at $299. **Don't go lower.**

### Handling "no"
- *"Too expensive"* → "Totally fair — Starter is $299 and you can cancel anytime. Want to try one free week and decide after?"
- *"I do it myself"* → "Makes sense! How much time does it take you each week? Most owners tell me it's 3–5 hours they'd rather spend on customers."
- *"Not right now"* → "No problem — mind if I check back in a month?" (Write it down and actually do it.)

## Delivering the work (per client, ~2 hrs/week)

1. Get access via **Meta Business Suite → Settings → Business assets → add people** (never ask for passwords).
2. Ask Claude each Sunday: *"Write 3 Instagram posts for [business] this week. Details: [specials, events, products]. Include captions, hashtags, and a description of the image to make."*
3. Make the graphics in Canva (free) using the descriptions — or take photos on your phone when you visit.
4. Schedule the week in Meta Business Suite (free built-in scheduler).
5. Check comments/DMs once a day and reply (ask Claude for replies if unsure).
6. End of month: screenshot the Insights page, paste the numbers into Claude, ask for a *"one-page plain-English monthly report."* Send it with the invoice.

---

## Money rules

- **Invoice on the 1st**, payment up front each month (Stripe/PayPal invoice).
- **Put every payment straight into a separate savings account** labeled "Hublot" — don't touch it.
- Keep a simple list of what you earned. If you earn over ~$400 in a year in the US, self-employment income may need to be reported on taxes — have the adult helping you check.
- When you're close: look at **Hublot boutiques, Chrono24, or authorized pre-owned dealers**. Only buy pre-owned from sellers that include authenticity papers and a return policy.
